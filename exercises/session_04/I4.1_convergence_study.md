## Instructions

The case is `EnergyTraining.Examples.S04_StorageCharging`: M03, 300 L at 30 °C, charged from the
top with 0.1 kg/s at 60 °C for 3 h. `simulate_variant` compiles it for any $N$ and returns the
result and the solver statistics.

1. **Part 1, number of nodes.** Complete `node_count_study` (one row per $N$). For
   $N$ = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 (DASSL, tolerance 1e-6) tabulate the outlet temperature after 1 h,
   the energy stored after 3 h and the time at which water above 55 °C first reaches the outlet;
   the plot of $T_{out}(t)$ for every $N$ is provided.
2. Which quantity converges quickly with $N$, and which does not? Choose $N$ for two purposes,
   and justify each choice in two sentences:
   - (a) the energy a heat pump puts into the tank in 3 h;
   - (b) the time at which water above 55 °C first reaches the outlet.
3. **Part 2, tolerance (after the session).** Complete `tolerance_study` for your $N$ of (a). Use DASSL tolerances
   1e-2 … 1e-10. Plot the largest error of $T_{out}$ against the Python reference and the number
   of right-hand-side evaluations. Is the error equal to the tolerance?
4. Compare the two errors: which one should you reduce first for this model?

The notebook runs OpenModelica from Python (local `omc` or the course Docker image); each $N$
is a new model, compiled in a few seconds.

## Extension activity

Repeat part 2 with `method="cvode"` and with explicit Euler (`method="euler"`, step = interval)
for 60 s, 30 s and 10 s. How small must the Euler step be to match the error of DASSL at
tolerance 1e-6, and how many right-hand-side evaluations does that cost?
